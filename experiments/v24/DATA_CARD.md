Training is Salesforce/wikitext, wikitext-2-raw-v1, train split, revision b08601e04326c79dfdd32d625aee71d232d685c3. Its 36,718 original rows are LF-joined then UTF-8 encoded (10,951,562 bytes), without normalization/filtering, as in the existing pinned v14 experiment. Source Wikipedia Good/Featured articles; card prose states CC BY-SA 4.0, while its metadata lists CC BY-SA 3.0. This upstream license-version inconsistency is unresolved; preserve attribution/share-alike obligations and do not redistribute corpus or weights. Pinned card: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/wikitext/blob/b08601e04326c79dfdd32d625aee71d232d685c3/README.md . Fixed random ledger seed 23112026 supplies 250 contiguous 2,048-input samples, sampled with replacement; each optimizer arm sees exactly 512,000 target presentations. No validation/test fitting. Byte vocabulary0–255, no learned tokenizer. These are byte targets, not commercial tokens.

Held-out evaluation is HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu, sample-10BT, train split used only for evaluation, revision87f09149ef4734204d70ed1d046ddc9ca3f2b8f9. This educationally filtered Common Crawl-derived public web corpus is a different source from the training set. Card states ODC-BY1.0 and Common Crawl terms also apply; underlying pages may retain third-party rights. Pinned card: https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu/blob/87f09149ef4734204d70ed1d046ddc9ca3f2b8f9/README.md . Public ungated access was verified, no new explicit legal acceptance or paid access used. Existing verification was rechecked by downloading both pinned cards; exact hashes and URLs are in data_audit.json.

Fresh viewer rows800–899, first4,097 UTF-8 bytes each; 100 documents/286,349 capped bytes. No content selection, normalization or concatenation. All eligible nonoverlapping 2,048-input windows score final128 next-byte targets. Nested512 resets to final512 inputs of each same window, retaining identical targets. This convenience sample is not a random representative corpus; conditioning on sufficient document length changes the cohort. The viewer endpoint itself is not immutable: x-revision is checked before use and downloaded raw/document hashes are retained, failing closed on drift.

Exact full-document duplicate checks found no within-cohort duplicates, no matches to previous v15–v23 full-document hash manifests, and no complete new document occurring in the full WikiText train serialization. This is not near-duplicate, passage overlap or exhaustive decontamination evidence. Training and evaluation remain operationally separate. No raw corpus text or checkpoints may be included in the research repository/archive. Checkpoints and rows.json are private scratch-only reproducibility inputs. Full source manifests retain upstream lineage and original training cost is not erased by continuation.
