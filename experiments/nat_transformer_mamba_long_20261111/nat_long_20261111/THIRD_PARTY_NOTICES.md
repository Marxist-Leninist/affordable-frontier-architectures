## Private source context and dependencies

User-owned private AGILLM source was inspected for architecture and NAT-objective context. It is not redistributed, and no public license for that private source is asserted. This experiment is not an exact production AGILLM implementation: production tokenizer, routing, quantization, scale, and mixed AR/SAT/NAT objectives are excluded. No existing repository license or access permissions are changed.

Dependencies are obtained from their normal distributions; their source or binaries are not included in the report bundle. Figures are generated with Matplotlib. Synthetic digit tasks are generated by the experiment.
