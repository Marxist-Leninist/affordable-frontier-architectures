# Economics: measurement boundary and retained gates

This is isolated already-available CPU scratch compute; no paid rental, purchase, funding or production changes. Incremental invoice is not observed, so neither actual cost per useful token nor zero resource cost is claimed. Byte-language-model outputs are not automatically commercial billable tokens. No matched frontier-quality claim is supported.

For hardware H USD/hour and productive byte throughput q, hardware cost per million produced bytes is 1e6 H/(3600 q u), where u is productive fraction of paid wall-clock time. Use u=1 only for continuous productive operation; do not divide by utilization again if q is already wall-clock averaged. Convert bytes into billable tokens with a measured workload-specific conversion before comparing token prices. Prefill is additional work, not free.

Retained target rates: $0.05/M uncached input, $0.001/M cached input, $0.10/M billable output. For monthly ledger I,C,O, gross nominal revenue R=(.05 I+.001 C+.10 O)/1e6 dollars. Net revenue N is R after applicable sales tax and payment fees, deducted once. Require total attributable cost <=0.70N for 30% margin on net revenue. Include separate prefill/decode occupancy, cache creation/hits/storage, memory, idle/burst/reliability headroom, storage/bandwidth, hidden non-billable reasoning/tool work, training amortization and operations. No measured full workload ledger, utilization, hardware rate or billable-token conversion exists for these tiny CPU models, so mixed-workload margin and cost gap are NOT evaluable.

Conditional output-only hardware floor: $0.10/M output and 30% margin leave $0.07/M costs. If this pays only hardware, with no other costs and 100% productive utilization, required aggregate service throughput is 3968.254*H billable output tokens/s; at productive utilization u, productive throughput must be 3968.254*H/u. This is not a universal mixed-workload gate, single-user speed or a measured result. Frontier-quality parity is an independent gate, unmet here.

For <=$500/month subscriptions apply the same 30% margin to net receipts while preserving the full agreed workload, quality, context, concurrency and latency. Even at $500 net with zero fees, total cost ceiling is $350/month. No prototype-to-frontier scaling assumption is defensible from these measurements. Training-control target counts and timings are reported separately; they do not prove an inference-price win.
